# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, inclusive and constructive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, role or technical approach.

This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, events and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- welcome different perspectives and constructive disagreement;
- focus criticism on ideas, code and behavior rather than people;
- protect confidential information and user privacy;
- follow the project's security and contribution rules;
- take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation or threats;
- personal attacks, insults or repeated unwanted contact;
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- doxxing, stalking or deliberate disruption of project spaces;
- sharing secrets, credentials, private links or user content;
- security testing against systems or data without authorization;
- retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting a concern

Please report concerns privately through the contact channel published in the repository metadata. Do not open a public issue for sensitive conduct reports.

Include only the information needed to understand the situation. Never include passwords, decryption keys, passphrases, complete share or management URLs, user content or personal data unless specifically requested through a secure channel.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may acknowledge the report, ask for clarification, take temporary protective measures, investigate, and communicate an outcome to the people involved when appropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, temporarily suspend access or permanently exclude a participant when necessary to protect the community. Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent removal from project spaces.

Retaliation, bad-faith reporting and attempts to expose a reporter's identity are violations of this Code of Conduct.

## Scope and attribution

This Code of Conduct applies within project repositories and whenever someone represents the project in public or private project-related spaces.

This document is inspired by common open-source community standards and may be adapted as the project and its community evolve.
